# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

The class page, calendar and roles now follow the website and the TIA TMS app.

- Class page in the website's layout: a summary card, then E-Attendance, Announcements, Virtual Class Link, Courseware, Lesson (topics, links, videos and quizzes), Course Survey and Certificate for learners; trainers get Announcements they can post and delete, quiz QR codes in the Lesson, Practice Exam (self-register QR) and the Course Feedback Summary.
- Classroom classes show their address (the academy's Woodlands address when the class has no other venue) instead of the word "Classroom".
- My Classes: "Find your course" search and status chips on every card.
- Calendar: a month grid with each day's classes, Today, swipe to change month and "Jump to next class".
- Support tab replaces Billing for learners: send feedback to the academy by email, or call, WhatsApp or find us. Billing moves into Account.
- Anyone with more than one role (Learner, Trainer, Admin, Marketing, Developer, Franchise Admin) can switch with "View as".
- Admin home: classes running today and in the next 30 days with KPI tiles and search; Admin calendar of every class. Developer home: the course list with upcoming classes. Marketing and Franchise Admin keep their dashboards. Staff get a Courses tab.

## [1.5] — 2026-11-21 · build 6

Quizzes come to TC LMS SG.

- Quizzes: when your trainer sets a quiz for a class, take it on the class page. Pick an answer for each question, submit, and see your score with the correct answers marked.
- Your latest score shows beside each quiz, and you can retake a quiz.

## [1.4] — 2026-11-19 · build 5

TC LMS SG now serves trainers as well as learners.

- Trainers can sign in: My Classes and Calendar show the classes you teach beside the classes you attend, tagged Teaching or Attending.
- Trainer class view: take attendance for each session, show a check-in QR code for learners to scan, open the trainer and learner materials, and see how many learners gave feedback.
- Class reminders: a notification 3 days before a class starts, the evening before each class day, and an hour before it begins. Turn them off in Account.
- Class materials and certificates open inside the app, with Share and Open in Safari.
- Edit Profile: update your name and phone number, and choose Light, Dark or System appearance. The choice is saved to your account.
- Dark mode throughout the app.
- Classes refresh automatically when you return to the app.

## [1.3] — 2026-11-04 · build 4

TC LMS SG — the learner app for Tertiary Courses Singapore (non-WSQ courses).

- Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your website password.
- My Classes: your current, upcoming and past classes with dates, times, venue or online link, and trainer.
- Digital attendance: check in to the current session from your phone.
- Class materials: learner slides, learner guide, lesson plan and labs for each class.
- In-house course feedback survey, right inside the app.
- Calendar of your class days, with "Add My Classes to Calendar".
- Certificates: view and share your certificates.
- Billing: your registrations, payment status and invoice numbers.
- Browse the non-WSQ course catalog and enquire on WhatsApp.
- In-app account deletion.
- New TC LMS SG app icon.
